# Video Production Workflow

## End-to-end process for creating video content at Vonas Media

### Overview Steps
- Pre-production planning and preparation
- Production execution and quality control  
- Post-production editing and finalization
- Distribution and promotion

### Content

**Pre-Production Phase**
- Guest selection and outreach
- Interview preparation and research
- Equipment setup and testing
- Location and technical requirements
- Content calendar scheduling

**Production Phase**
- Recording execution
- Quality monitoring during recording
- Multiple angle capture (if applicable)
- Real-time issue resolution

**Post-Production Phase**
- Raw footage review and organization
- Video editing and enhancement
- Audio optimization
- Graphics and branding integration
- Final review and approval

**Distribution Phase**
- Platform-specific formatting
- Upload scheduling
- Metadata optimization
- Promotional content creation
