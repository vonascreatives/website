# Guest Database Management in Airtable - Complete Guide

## Description
Comprehensive guide for managing guest information, automating form distribution, and streamlining the guest onboarding process using Airtable automation for VONAS Media shows.

2. Guide guests through initial form completion and information gathering

4. Process release forms and legal documentation efficiently
5. Monitor guest pipeline and maintain organized database records

### Guest Database Workflow Process

#### **Step 1: Initial Guest Form Completion**
**Guest Entry Point:**
- **Form URL**: https://airtable.com/appetFsttlj6iXczF/pag7Uw29ubwrDGbb0/form
- **Information Collected**: Basic guest details, contact information, background information
- **Purpose**: Establish initial guest profile and contact database entry

**Form Submission Triggers:**
Upon form submission, the system automatically:
1. Creates new record in Guest Backlog table
2. Generates prefill link for additional information
3. Creates shortened link for easy sharing
4. Triggers automated email sequence

#### **Step 2: Automated Prefill Link Generation**
**System Automation Process:**
- **Automatic Generation**: Prefill link and short link created immediately upon submission
- **Link Purpose**: Pre-populated form for guest to complete additional required information
- **Information Integration**: Links guest to specific show participation and detailed requirements
- **Time Efficiency**: Eliminates manual link creation and reduces processing time

**Link Distribution Method:**
- Automated email sent to guest's provided email address
- Email includes prefill link and instructions for completion
- Clear guidance on next steps and timeline expectations
- Professional communication maintaining VONAS Media brand standards

#### **Step 3: Guest Information Completion**
**Required Additional Information:**
- **Show Selection**: Guest must specify which show they will participate in
- **Detailed Background**: Additional information relevant to show format and content
- **Availability Scheduling**: Recording dates and time preferences
- **Technical Requirements**: Equipment needs, location preferences, special accommodations

**Automated Processing:**
Upon completion of additional information:
1. Record automatically created in Guest Backlog (Updates) table
2. Information integrated with existing guest profile
3. Release form email automatically triggered and sent
4. Guest status updated in database tracking system

#### **Step 4: Release Form Distribution and Collection**
**Automated Release Form Process:**
- **Email Trigger**: Automatic email sent upon information form completion
- **Release Form Access**: Email contains download link for legal release form
- **Upload Instructions**: Clear directions for completing and returning signed form
- **Upload Link**: Dedicated link provided for PDF release form submission

**Release Form Requirements:**
- **Format**: PDF document required for legal compliance
- **Completion**: All fields must be filled out completely and accurately
- **Signature**: Legal signature required (digital or scanned physical signature)
- **Upload**: Submission through provided secure upload link

#### **Step 5: Final Processing and Database Update**
**Completion Verification:**
Upon release form submission:
1. **Database Update**: Release form appears in Release Form field in Guest Backlog (Updates)
2. **Record Completion**: Guest profile marked as complete with all required documentation
3. **Production Ready**: Guest cleared for scheduling and production coordination
4. **Legal Compliance**: All required legal documentation collected and stored securely

### Database Structure and Organization

#### **Primary Tables and Functions**
**Guest Backlog Table:**
- **Purpose**: Initial guest contact information and basic details
- **Automation**: Prefill link generation and initial email trigger
- **Information**: Contact details, basic background, initial interest indication

**Guest Backlog (Updates) Table:**
- **Purpose**: Complete guest information with show-specific details
- **Automation**: Release form email trigger and legal document management
- **Information**: Detailed background, show assignment, availability, technical requirements

**Release Form Management:**
- **Storage**: PDF release forms automatically stored in appropriate database fields
- **Organization**: Forms linked to specific guest records for easy access and legal compliance
- **Security**: Secure storage ensuring privacy and legal protection
