# Intern Onboarding Checklist

## Description
Comprehensive onboarding process for new interns covering administrative tasks, training modules, system access, and integration into VONAS Media culture and workflows.

## Steps
1. Complete pre-onboarding preparation and documentation
2. Execute first-day orientation and introductions  
3. Provide system access and training
4. Assign initial tasks and mentorship
5. Schedule check-ins and evaluation milestones

## Content

### Pre-Onboarding (Before Day 1)
- **Documentation Review**
  - Contract signing and legal documents
  - Personal information and payroll forms
  - Emergency contact information
  - NDA and confidentiality agreements

- **System Preparation**
  - Email account creation
  - Slack workspace invitation
  - Database access permissions
  - Tool licenses and accounts

### Day 1 Orientation
- **Welcome and Introductions**
  - Meet the team and key contacts
  - Office/workspace tour (if applicable)
  - Company culture presentation
  - Role expectations and objectives

- **Administrative Setup**
  - IT equipment and access
  - Login credentials for all systems
  - Calendar integration and scheduling
  - Contact list and org chart review

### Week 1-2: Foundation Training
- **Core Systems Training**
  - Slack communication protocols
  - Project management tools (SmartSuite/Airtable)
  - Content creation software
  - File organization and storage

- **Role-Specific Training**
  - Department-specific workflows
  - Quality standards and guidelines
  - Reporting and communication requirements
  - Initial task assignments

### Month 1: Integration and Development
- **Skill Development**
  - Industry-specific training modules
  - Software proficiency building
  - Best practices workshops
  - Feedback and improvement areas

- **Performance Evaluation**
  - 30-day review meeting
  - Goal setting and adjustments
  - Mentor feedback collection
  - Development plan creation

### Ongoing Support
- **Regular Check-ins**
  - Weekly one-on-ones
  - Monthly progress reviews
  - Quarterly goal assessments
  - End-of-internship evaluation

## Checklist
- [ ] Contract and legal documents completed
- [ ] System access configured
- [ ] Team introductions completed
- [ ] Core training modules finished
- [ ] Initial tasks assigned
- [ ] Mentor assigned and introduced
- [ ] 30-day review scheduled
- [ ] Performance metrics established
- [ ] Feedback mechanisms in place
- [ ] Offboarding process documented
