# Content Approval Standard Operating Procedure

## Description
Standardized process for reviewing, approving, and publishing all VONAS Media content including quality control checkpoints, approval workflows, and final publication procedures.

## Steps
1. Submit content for initial review with all required materials
2. Complete technical and content quality review process
3. Obtain necessary approvals from stakeholders and legal
4. Make required revisions and re-submit if needed
5. Publish content following distribution protocols

## Content

### Pre-Submission Requirements

#### Content Preparation Checklist
- **Technical Specifications Met**: Resolution, audio quality, file formats
- **Brand Guidelines Applied**: Visual identity, typography, color schemes
- **Legal Requirements**: Releases, permissions, copyright clearances
- **Metadata Complete**: Titles, descriptions, tags, credits
- **Supporting Materials**: Show notes, social media assets, thumbnails

#### Required Documentation
- **Content Brief**: Original concept and objectives
- **Guest Releases**: Signed appearance and usage agreements
- **Music Licensing**: Rights clearance for any music used
- **Image Permissions**: Rights for photos, graphics, or B-roll
- **Fact-Check Documentation**: Verification of claims and statistics

### Review Process Workflow

#### Stage 1: Technical Review (24-48 hours)
**Reviewer**: Post-Production Team
- **Audio Quality**: Levels, clarity, sync issues
- **Video Quality**: Resolution, color correction, stability
- **Edit Quality**: Pacing, transitions, graphics integration
- **Format Compliance**: File specifications for distribution
- **Asset Organization**: Proper file naming and organization

**Approval Criteria**:
- ✅ Technical standards met
- ✅ Brand guidelines followed
- ✅ Edit quality acceptable
- ❌ Requires technical revision

#### Stage 2: Content Review (48-72 hours)
**Reviewer**: Content Director + Show Producer
- **Editorial Quality**: Story flow, engagement, clarity
- **Brand Alignment**: Consistent with VONAS values and positioning
- **Audience Appropriateness**: Suitable for target demographic
- **Legal Compliance**: No copyright, defamation, or privacy issues
- **Factual Accuracy**: Claims verified and accurate

**Approval Criteria**:
- ✅ Content quality excellent
- ✅ Brand alignment confirmed
- ✅ Legal issues cleared
- ⚠️ Minor revisions needed
- ❌ Major revisions required

#### Stage 3: Final Approval (24 hours)
**Reviewer**: Executive Producer or Leadership
- **Strategic Alignment**: Fits overall content strategy
- **Quality Standards**: Meets VONAS Media excellence standards
- **Publication Readiness**: All requirements satisfied
- **Distribution Planning**: Channels and timing confirmed

**Approval Options**:
- ✅ **Approved for Publication**: Ready for immediate release
- ⚠️ **Conditional Approval**: Minor changes required before publication
- ❌ **Not Approved**: Significant revisions needed, return to previous stage

### Revision and Re-submission Process

#### Minor Revisions (Stage 2 or 3 feedback)
- **Timeline**: 24-48 hours for completion
- **Scope**: Small edits, adjustments, or additions
- **Re-review**: Same reviewer, expedited process
- **Communication**: Direct feedback with specific change requests

#### Major Revisions (Stage 2 feedback)
- **Timeline**: 3-5 business days for completion
- **Scope**: Significant content changes or re-editing
- **Re-review**: Full review cycle starting at Stage 1
- **Meeting Required**: Discussion of changes and expectations

#### Rejected Content (Stage 2 or 3)
- **Review Meeting**: Scheduled within 48 hours
- **Issue Analysis**: Identify specific problems and solutions
- **Decision**: Revise, postpone, or cancel content
- **Documentation**: Record reasons and learnings for future

### Emergency and Rush Approval Process

#### Criteria for Emergency Process
- **Time-sensitive Content**: News, events, trending topics
- **Technical Emergencies**: Platform issues, urgent corrections
- **Business Critical**: High-priority partnerships or opportunities

#### Expedited Workflow
- **Notification**: All reviewers notified of emergency status
- **Timeline**: 4-hour maximum for each review stage
- **Communication**: Real-time collaboration and feedback
- **Quality Assurance**: Reduced review scope, focus on critical elements

### Publication and Distribution

#### Pre-Publication Final Check
- **File Verification**: Correct version and format
- **Metadata Review**: Titles, descriptions, tags accurate
- **Thumbnail Quality**: Eye-catching and brand-compliant
- **Distribution List**: Confirmed channels and timing
- **Promotional Assets**: Social media content ready

#### Publication Protocol
1. **Primary Platform Upload**: YouTube, podcast platforms
2. **Metadata Application**: SEO-optimized descriptions and tags
3. **Thumbnail Assignment**: High-quality, engaging thumbnails
4. **Social Media Scheduling**: Coordinated promotional posts
5. **Team Notification**: Inform all stakeholders of publication

#### Post-Publication Monitoring
- **Performance Tracking**: Initial engagement metrics
- **Error Identification**: Technical issues or content problems
- **Audience Feedback**: Comments, messages, reviews
- **Correction Protocol**: Process for addressing post-publication issues
