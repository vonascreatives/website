## Content

### Company Overview
VONAS Media is a dynamic Filipino digital media production company specializing in multiple YouTube channels and podcast formats. The company operates five distinct show brands, each targeting different audiences and content niches within the Filipino market while maintaining international appeal.

### Show Portfolio

#### **Off The Record (OTR)**
A show dedicated to featuring individuals often marginalized by society, including rebels, those involved in controversial activities, and individuals with extraordinary life stories. The core value is to look beyond surface judgments and explore the depth of human experience. Currently expanding with OTR 2.0 focusing on documentary-style content with freelance writers and researchers.

#### **Skyline Music** 
Premiere destination for discovering vibrant and diverse sounds of the Filipino indie music scene. Features exclusive artist spotlights, captivating live sessions, and in-depth interviews with Filipino indie talents and hidden gems.

#### **Tatak**
Highlights small and independent businesses, focusing on handcrafted products from the Philippines. Showcases diverse handmade items by small family-owned businesses or communities, celebrating artistry, uniqueness, and cultural heritage.

#### **At The Backdoor**
Friendly hangout spot diving into the heart and soul of Filipino youth culture. Covers latest trends, deep dives into rich traditions, and what's buzzing among young Filipinos.

#### **Skyline Slam**
Premier battle rap competition in the Philippines showcasing the best rap talents from various cities. Participants compete in multiple rounds with creative and powerful lyrical performances, promoting Filipino hip-hop culture.
