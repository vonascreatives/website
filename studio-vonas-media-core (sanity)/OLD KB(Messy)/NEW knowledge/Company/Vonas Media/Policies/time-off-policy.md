# Time Off and Leave Policy

## Description
Comprehensive policy covering vacation time, sick leave, personal days, and other time-off requests including approval procedures, coverage requirements, and emergency leave protocols.

## Steps
1. Determine type of leave needed and eligibility
2. Submit request using proper channels and timeline
3. Arrange coverage and handoff responsibilities
4. Get approval from supervisor and HR
5. Document time off and ensure smooth transition

## Content

### Types of Leave Available

#### Paid Time Off (PTO)
- **Vacation Days**: 15 days annually (increases with tenure)
- **Personal Days**: 3 days annually for personal matters
- **Sick Days**: 10 days annually for health-related issues
- **Holiday Leave**: Standard holidays plus floating holidays

#### Extended Leave Options
- **Bereavement Leave**: Up to 5 days for immediate family
- **Jury Duty**: Required court appearances (unpaid but job protected)
- **Medical Leave**: Extended illness or medical procedures
- **Family Leave**: Birth, adoption, or family care (follows FMLA guidelines)

### Request and Approval Process

#### Standard PTO Requests
1. **Advance Notice Requirements**
   - 2 weeks minimum for vacation requests
   - 1 week minimum for personal days
   - Same-day notification acceptable for sick leave

2. **Submission Process**
   - Use company HR portal or email request
   - Include specific dates and reason for leave
   - Identify coverage arrangements
   - Await supervisor and HR approval

3. **Approval Criteria**
   - Business needs and project deadlines
   - Adequate coverage available
   - Fair distribution of time off
   - Previous approval commitments

#### Emergency and Sick Leave
- **Immediate Notification**: Call or text supervisor ASAP
- **Follow-up Documentation**: Submit formal request within 24 hours
- **Medical Documentation**: May be required for extended sick leave
- **Return to Work**: Medical clearance may be required

### Coverage and Handoff Procedures

#### Before Taking Leave
- **Project Status Update**: Document all ongoing projects
- **Task Delegation**: Assign responsibilities to team members
- **Client Communication**: Inform clients of temporary absence
- **Emergency Contacts**: Provide contacts for urgent matters
- **Access and Passwords**: Share necessary system access

#### During Leave
- **Limited Contact**: Emergencies only unless otherwise arranged
- **Response Expectations**: No expectation to respond to routine matters
- **Coverage Support**: Brief check-ins only if absolutely necessary

#### Return from Leave
- **Status Briefings**: Receive updates on projects and changes
- **Priority Review**: Identify urgent matters requiring attention
- **Smooth Transition**: Resume responsibilities gradually if needed

### Special Considerations

#### Peak Season Restrictions
- **High-Demand Periods**: Limited approvals during busy seasons
- **Advance Planning**: Extra advance notice required
- **Alternative Dates**: Flexibility encouraged for rescheduling
- **Fair Distribution**: Rotating approval system during conflicts

#### Remote Work and Flexible Arrangements
- **Working Vacation**: Partial work arrangements possible
- **Time Zone Considerations**: Adjusted schedules for travel
- **Technology Requirements**: Ensure reliable internet if working remotely
- **Boundary Setting**: Clear expectations for availability
