# Production FAQs

## Description
Comprehensive frequently asked questions covering all aspects of VONAS Media production including pre-production, recording, post-production, and technical requirements.

## Content

### Pre-Production Questions

#### Q: How far in advance should we schedule recordings?
**A:** Ideally 2-4 weeks in advance for standard episodes, 4-6 weeks for high-profile guests or special episodes. This allows adequate time for research, preparation, and coordination. Rush recordings can be accommodated with 48-72 hours notice but may require premium scheduling.

#### Q: What information do we need from guests before recording?
**A:** Essential information includes: full name and preferred pronunciation, current title and company, bio/background for introduction, high-resolution headshot, any technical requirements, dietary restrictions (for in-studio), and signed appearance release form.

#### Q: How long does the pre-production process typically take?
**A:** Complete pre-production typically requires 5-7 business days including guest research, question development, technical coordination, and final preparation. Complex episodes or first-time guests may require additional time.

#### Q: What happens if a guest cancels at the last minute?
**A:** We maintain a backup guest list and can often reschedule within 24-48 hours. Cancellation policies vary by show format, but we always work to minimize disruption and accommodate both guest needs and production schedules.

### Recording and Technical Questions

#### Q: How long do recording sessions typically last?
**A:** Standard podcast recordings run 45-90 minutes depending on show format. This includes pre-recording setup (15 minutes), the conversation itself (45-75 minutes), and post-recording wrap-up (10 minutes). Complex multi-segment recordings may require 2-3 hours.

#### Q: What equipment do we use for recording?
**A:** We use professional-grade equipment including multiple 4K cameras, broadcast-quality microphones, professional lighting rigs, and industry-standard recording software. All equipment is regularly maintained and calibrated for optimal performance.

#### Q: Can we record remotely with guests?
**A:** Yes, we have professional remote recording capabilities using high-quality software and provide technical support to ensure broadcast-quality remote recordings. We conduct tech tests 15-30 minutes before recording to ensure optimal setup.

#### Q: What backup systems do we have in place?
**A:** Multiple backup systems including redundant recording devices, cloud backup during recording, separate audio recording devices, and backup power systems. We maintain 99%+ recording success rate through comprehensive redundancy.

#### Q: What should I do if there's a technical issue during recording?
**A:** Immediately signal the technical team (hand signals for audio issues). We can pause recording to address issues without losing content. Most technical issues can be resolved within 2-3 minutes with proper backup systems in place.

### Post-Production Questions

#### Q: How long does post-production take?
**A:** Standard post-production timeline is 5-7 business days for full episodes. This includes editing, color correction, audio enhancement, graphics integration, and quality control. Rush jobs can be completed in 2-3 days with advance notice and premium rates.

#### Q: Can we make changes after post-production is complete?
**A:** Minor changes can be accommodated within 24 hours of delivery at no cost. Major revisions may require additional time and costs depending on scope. All changes should be consolidated into a single revision request when possible.

#### Q: What formats do we deliver in?
**A:** Standard deliverables include: master archive file (ProRes), streaming optimized file (H.264), podcast audio version (WAV and MP3), social media clips (various aspect ratios), and guest copy in requested format.

#### Q: How do we ensure consistent quality across all episodes?
**A:** We maintain detailed quality control checklists, standardized templates and presets, regular calibration of all equipment, and multi-stage review processes. Each episode undergoes technical and content review before final delivery.

### Guest and Content Questions

#### Q: How do we select guests for different shows?
**A:** Guest selection varies by show format but generally includes: relevance to target audience, compelling personal or professional story, media experience and comfort level, alignment with show values, and availability within production timeline.

#### Q: What if a guest wants to review the content before publication?
**A:** Guest review is available for sensitive content or high-profile guests. We provide rough cuts within 48 hours of recording and accommodate reasonable feedback. Final approval is typically required within 3-5 business days.

#### Q: How do we handle controversial or sensitive topics?
**A:** We have established protocols for sensitive content including legal review, fact-checking processes, and guest consent procedures. All potentially controversial content undergoes additional review stages and stakeholder approval.

#### Q: Can guests promote their products or services during the show?
**A:** Promotional content guidelines vary by show format. Generally, organic mentions and brief promotions are acceptable if they add value to the conversation. Excessive promotion is edited out to maintain content quality and audience engagement.

### Workflow and Coordination Questions

#### Q: How do we coordinate between different team members during production?
**A:** We use Slack for real-time communication, project management systems for task tracking, shared calendars for scheduling, and regular team meetings for coordination. Clear role definitions and communication protocols ensure smooth operations.

#### Q: What happens if multiple shows are being produced simultaneously?
**A:** Our production pipeline can handle multiple concurrent projects through careful scheduling, resource allocation, and priority management. Each show has dedicated team members while sharing resources for efficiency.

#### Q: How do we track production progress and deadlines?
**A:** We use project management software to track all production phases, automated deadline reminders, progress dashboards, and regular status meetings. Stakeholders receive regular updates on production status and any potential delays.

#### Q: What's the process for rush or urgent content?
**A:** Rush content requires approval from production management, premium rates apply, all stakeholders must be notified of compressed timeline, quality standards remain unchanged, and additional resources may be allocated to meet deadlines.

### Quality and Standards Questions

#### Q: What are our minimum technical standards?
**A:** Minimum standards include: 1080p video resolution, broadcast-quality audio (-23 LUFS), professional color correction, consistent branding application, and multi-format compatibility. Higher standards apply for premium shows like Skyline.

#### Q: How do we maintain consistency across different operators?
**A:** Comprehensive training programs, detailed SOPs and checklists, regular quality audits, peer review processes, and ongoing feedback systems ensure consistency regardless of individual operators.

#### Q: What's our policy on retakes or re-recording?
**A:** Retakes are generally avoided through proper preparation and professional execution. When necessary, partial re-recording is preferred over complete retakes. Costs and scheduling implications are discussed with stakeholders before proceeding.

#### Q: How do we handle content that doesn't meet our standards?
**A:** Substandard content undergoes additional editing or enhancement. If standards cannot be met, we may recommend re-recording portions or, in rare cases, not publishing the content. Quality standards are never compromised for timeline convenience.

### Business and Legal Questions

#### Q: What legal releases and permissions do we need?
**A:** Required documentation includes: guest appearance releases, music licensing agreements, location permits (if applicable), photography releases for any images used, and copyright clearances for any third-party content.

#### Q: Who owns the content after production?
**A:** VONAS Media retains ownership of produced content with guests receiving rights for personal promotional use. Specific ownership terms are outlined in guest agreements and partnership contracts.

#### Q: What's our policy on content distribution and syndication?
**A:** Content is distributed across VONAS Media platforms and partner networks as outlined in agreements. Syndication opportunities are evaluated on a case-by-case basis with revenue sharing terms clearly defined.

#### Q: How do we handle content takedown requests?
**A:** Takedown requests are reviewed by legal and content teams, evaluated based on contractual obligations and legal requirements, and handled according to established protocols while balancing all stakeholder interests.

### Troubleshooting and Support Questions

#### Q: Who do I contact for technical support during production?
**A:** Technical support is available through dedicated Slack channels, direct phone lines during production hours, and on-site technical team members. Emergency technical support is available 24/7 for urgent issues.

#### Q: What's the escalation process for production issues?
**A:** Issues escalate from producer to production manager to executive producer based on severity and complexity. Clear escalation criteria and contact information ensure rapid resolution of critical issues.

#### Q: How do we handle equipment failures or technical emergencies?
**A:** Backup equipment is always on-site, emergency vendor contacts are maintained for rapid replacement, mobile production kits are available for quick deployment, and all technical team members are trained in emergency procedures.
