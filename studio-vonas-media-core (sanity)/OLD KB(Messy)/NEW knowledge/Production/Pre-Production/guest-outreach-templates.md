# Guest Outreach Email Templates

## Description  
Professional email templates and communication strategies for reaching out to potential podcast and video guests, including initial contact, follow-up sequences, and booking confirmation processes.

## Steps
1. Research and identify target guests
2. Personalize outreach using appropriate template
3. Send initial contact email with clear value proposition
4. Execute follow-up sequence based on response
5. Confirm booking details and logistics

## Content

### Initial Outreach Template

**Subject**: Invitation to Share Your Story on [Show Name]

Hi [Guest Name],

I hope this email finds you well. My name is [Your Name] and I'm reaching out from VONAS Media regarding our [Show Name] podcast/video series.

**Why You**: We've been following your work at [Company/Organization] and are particularly impressed by [specific achievement or project]. Your expertise in [relevant field] aligns perfectly with our audience's interests.

**About the Show**: [Show Name] features [brief show description] and has reached [audience metrics] with guests including [notable past guests].

**What We're Offering**:
- Professional production quality
- Full edited video and audio content
- Cross-platform distribution
- Social media promotional support
- Flexible recording options (remote/in-studio)

**Time Commitment**: The conversation typically lasts 45-60 minutes, with minimal preparation required.

Would you be interested in exploring this opportunity? I'd be happy to discuss the details and answer any questions.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Contact Information]

### Follow-Up Template 1 (Week 1)

**Subject**: Following up - [Show Name] Invitation

Hi [Guest Name],

I wanted to circle back on my previous email regarding the [Show Name] opportunity. 

I understand you're likely managing a busy schedule. To make this as convenient as possible, we offer:
- Flexible recording times (including evenings/weekends)
- Remote recording capabilities
- All questions provided in advance
- Quick 30-minute option if preferred

Here's a sample of our recent episode with [similar guest] to give you a feel for the format: [link]

Would any of these options work better for your schedule?

Thanks for your consideration.
[Your Name]

### Follow-Up Template 2 (Week 3)  

**Subject**: Last Follow-up - [Show Name] Collaboration

Hi [Guest Name],

This will be my final follow-up regarding the [Show Name] interview opportunity.

If timing isn't right now, I completely understand - please feel free to reach out if circumstances change in the future.

If you know someone in your network who might be interested in sharing their story, I'd appreciate the referral.

Thank you for your time and continued excellent work at [Company/Organization].

Best,
[Your Name]

### Booking Confirmation Template

**Subject**: Confirmed: [Show Name] Recording - [Date/Time]

Hi [Guest Name],

Excited to confirm your appearance on [Show Name]!

**Recording Details**:
- Date: [Date]
- Time: [Time with timezone]
- Duration: [Expected length]
- Format: [Remote/In-studio]
- Platform: [Zoom/Studio location]

**Preparation**:
- Tech check: [Time] (optional but recommended)
- Question themes: [Attached/Listed below]
- What to wear: [Guidelines]
- Backup contact: [Phone number]

**What You'll Receive**:
- Full edited video within 5-7 business days
- Audio podcast version
- Social media clips
- Professional photos from session

Looking forward to our conversation!

[Your Name]
[Contact Details]
