## Content

### Faceless Video Format Overview

#### **Behind the Hype Format**
**Target Specifications:**
- Duration: Under 59 seconds maximum
- Time Limit: 1 hour per video maximum
- Format: TikTok (9:16 vertical, HD resolution)
- Style: Disney-style character footage with high energy

**Content Requirements:**
- Disney-style character animations mandatory
- Fast-paced editing with energetic cuts
- Upbeat, powerful, serious, urban/pop music
- High energy maintained throughout entire video
- Professional voice-over with designated Voice ID

#### **Tiny Worker Format**
**Target Specifications:**
- Duration: Under 59 seconds maximum
- Time Limit: 2 hours per video maximum
- Format: TikTok (9:16 vertical, HD resolution)
- Style: Cinematic with film grain and effects

**Special Effects Requirements:**
- Film grain: Black and white grain (visible and organic)
- Focus: Tilt/shift effect application
- Color boost: Enhanced color across all clips
- Sound design: Strategic sound effect placement throughout video

#### **Hybrid Hunters Format**
**Target Specifications:**
- Duration: 20-44 seconds each
- Time Limit: 10 minutes per video maximum (speed prioritized)
- Format: TikTok (9:16 vertical, HD resolution)
- Style: Quick, efficient production with micro-sound integration

#### **Sport Savvy Format**
**Target Specifications:**
- Duration: Under 59 seconds
- Time Limit: 1-2 minutes per video maximum
- Format: TikTok (9:16 vertical, HD resolution)
- Style: Sports-focused content with dynamic editing

#### **AI UGC (User Generated Content) Format**
**Target Specifications:**
- Platform integration: Etsy/Amazon product focus
- HeyGen avatar integration: "Leia" character in outdoor settings
- Template preference: Simple or Floating layouts
- Music selection: "Neptune" recommended background music

### Production Workflows by Format

#### **Behind the Hype Production Workflow**
**Step 1: Asset Preparation**
1. Access Airtable project management and locate assigned video
2. Gather Disney-style character footage from organized folders
3. Select clips matching voice-over narrative requirements
4. Prepare high-energy music tracks from Artlist (example: "Dripper")

**Step 2: Editing Process**
1. Import selected Disney-style footage ensuring character consistency
2. Sync footage with provided voice-over audio
3. Apply fast-paced editing style maintaining high energy throughout
4. Integrate multiple music tracks avoiding single-song dependency
5. Add strategic sound effects enhancing impact and engagement

**Step 3: Quality Control and Delivery**
1. Ensure video remains under 59-second limit
2. Export in 9:16 vertical format with HD resolution
3. Update Airtable with duration and edit time documentation
4. Upload to designated Google Drive folder with proper naming convention

#### **Tiny Worker Production Workflow**
**Step 1: Cinematic Setup**
1. Select appropriate footage for cinematic treatment
2. Review example video for style reference and consistency
3. Plan effect application sequence for optimal visual impact

**Step 2: Effect Application**
1. **Film Grain**: Apply black and white grain ensuring visibility and organic feel
2. **Focus Effects**: Implement tilt/shift focus for cinematic depth
3. **Color Enhancement**: Apply color boost across all clips with optimized settings
4. **Sound Design**: Strategic placement of sound effects throughout video

**Step 3: Music and Audio**
1. Select sad, powerful, warm-hearted music tracks from Artlist (example: "Leaves")
2. Layer multiple tracks avoiding single-song reliance
3. Integrate sound design elements as critical component
4. Balance audio levels ensuring voice-over clarity

#### **AI UGC Production Workflow**
**Step 1: HeyGen Setup and Product Integration**
1. Access HeyGen platform and paste product link from Etsy/Amazon
2. Allow processing time while working on other tasks simultaneously
3. Maintain English language setting (or Filipino if specifically required)
4. Select TikTok platform option for optimal formatting

**Step 2: Avatar and Template Configuration**
1. **Avatar Selection**: Choose "Leia" character for consistent branding
2. **Setting Preference**: Select outdoor settings for better product visibility
3. **Template Choice**: Use "Simple" or "Floating" with Simple recommended
4. **Music Integration**: Apply "Neptune" background music or experiment with alternatives

**Step 3: Content Enhancement and Delivery**
1. Copy enhanced voice-over version for more engaging delivery
2. Replace original text with enhanced version maintaining quality
3. Generate final video and download with proper file naming
4. Organize completed videos in dated project folders with specific naming convention

### Quality Standards and Optimization

#### **Universal Quality Requirements**
**Technical Standards:**
- All formats require 9:16 vertical aspect ratio
- HD resolution minimum across all productions
- Professional audio quality with clear voice-over
- Smooth transitions and consistent pacing
- Proper export settings for platform optimization

**Content Standards:**
- Engaging opening hooks within first 3 seconds
- Consistent energy and pacing throughout duration
- Brand-appropriate music and sound effect integration
- Clear, intelligible voice-over synchronized with visuals
- Platform-optimized formatting and presentation

#### **Efficiency Optimization Strategies**
**Time Management:**
- Behind the Hype: Maximum 1 hour per video
- Tiny Worker: Maximum 2 hours per video
- Hybrid Hunters: Maximum 10 minutes per video (speed prioritized)
- Sport Savvy: Maximum 1-2 minutes per video
- Batch processing when possible for similar content types

**Asset Reuse and Organization:**
- Maintain organized asset libraries for quick access
- Reuse high-quality character footage across appropriate projects
- Develop template systems for consistent formatting
- Create preset configurations for commonly used effects and settings

### File Organization and Delivery Systems

**Project Folder Structure:**
- Format: `day_month_project_name`
- Examples: `01_12_Digital_Templates`, `15_03_Kitchen_Gadgets`, `28_07_Fitness_Equipment`
- Consistent application across all faceless video formats

**Individual File Naming:**
- Match Airtable entry naming exactly for tracking consistency
- Include format type identifier when managing multiple formats
- Date stamp for version control and organization
- Clear, descriptive names avoiding ambiguity

#### **Delivery and Upload Procedures**
**Designated Upload Locations:**
- Behind the Hype: Specific Google Drive folder for high-energy content
- Tiny Worker: Designated folder for cinematic-style videos
- Hybrid Hunters: Quick-turnaround content folder
- Sport Savvy: Sports content specific directory
- AI UGC: Product-focused content repository

**Documentation Requirements:**
- Update Airtable project status with completion confirmation
- Record actual time spent versus target time limits
- Note any challenges or optimization opportunities
- Mark projects as "Edit Complete" for workflow tracking

### Troubleshooting and Problem Resolution

#### **Common Technical Issues**
**Audio Synchronization Problems:**
- Check original voice-over file quality and format compatibility
- Verify timeline settings match audio specifications
- Apply manual sync adjustment when automated sync fails
- Use backup audio sources when primary files corrupted

**Asset Access Issues:**
- Verify folder permissions and shared drive connectivity
- Check for updated folder locations or organizational changes
- Communicate with team for asset access resolution
- Maintain local backup copies of frequently used assets

#### **Quality Control Checkpoints**
**Pre-Upload Verification:**
- Duration compliance with format-specific requirements
- Aspect ratio and resolution confirmation
- Audio levels and quality verification
- Visual consistency and effect application check
- Brand guideline adherence confirmation
