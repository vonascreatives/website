# Off The Record (OTR) Show Format Guide

## Description
Comprehensive production guide for Off The Record show format including unique requirements, content guidelines, guest criteria, and production workflows specific to this show's intimate conversational style.

## Steps
1. Understand OTR's unique format and positioning
2. Apply guest selection criteria specific to OTR
3. Set up intimate recording environment
4. Execute conversational interview style
5. Follow OTR-specific post-production workflow

## Content

### Show Concept and Positioning
Off The Record provides an intimate platform for authentic conversations with individuals who have compelling personal stories, unique perspectives, or significant achievements they're ready to share openly.

**Core Elements**:
- Intimate, conversational atmosphere
- Personal story focus over business promotion
- Authentic, unscripted dialogue
- Behind-the-scenes perspective
- Trust-based environment encouraging vulnerability

### Unique Format Requirements

#### Pre-Production
- **Guest Vetting Process**
  - In-depth background research
  - Pre-interview conversation (15-30 minutes)
  - Story verification and fact-checking
  - Comfort level assessment
  - Clear expectation setting

#### Recording Setup
- **Environment**
  - Intimate setting (studio or controlled environment)
  - Warm, comfortable seating arrangement  
  - Minimal technical distractions
  - Professional but approachable atmosphere

- **Technical Requirements**
  - High-quality audio (primary focus)
  - Multiple camera angles for intimacy
  - Backup recording systems
  - Comfortable microphone setup

### Content Guidelines and Restrictions

#### Encouraged Content
- Personal journey and growth stories
- Challenging experiences and lessons learned
- Authentic emotions and vulnerability
- Behind-the-scenes industry insights
- Unique perspectives on common experiences

#### Content Restrictions
- No promotional pitches or sales content
- Avoid overly sensitive or traumatic details without consent
- Respect privacy boundaries of third parties
- No unverified claims or allegations
- Professional discretion on controversial topics

### Guest Criteria

#### Ideal Guest Profile
- Compelling personal or professional story
- Comfortable with authentic conversation
- Willing to share genuinely and openly
- Interesting perspective or unique experience
- Aligns with show's intimate format

#### Guest Preparation
- Pre-interview comfort conversation
- Clear explanation of format and expectations
- Discussion of boundaries and comfort zones
- Question theme overview (not specific questions)
- Consent and release form completion

### Production Workflow

#### Recording Process
1. **Pre-Recording** (15 minutes)
   - Final tech check and comfort assessment
   - Brief conversation to ease nerves
   - Final boundary confirmation
   
2. **Recording** (45-90 minutes)
   - Natural conversation flow
   - Follow interesting tangents
   - Allow for pauses and reflection
   - Maintain intimate atmosphere
   
3. **Post-Recording** (10 minutes)
   - Guest feedback and comfort check
   - Discussion of final edit preferences
   - Next steps and timeline communication

#### Post-Production Specific to OTR
- **Editing Philosophy**
  - Preserve authentic moments and pauses
  - Remove only technical issues or guest requests
  - Maintain conversational flow and natural rhythm
  - Enhance audio quality without losing intimacy

- **Content Review**
  - Guest approval process for sensitive content
  - Fact-checking of shared stories
  - Legal review if necessary
  - Final guest sign-off before publication

### Distribution Strategy
- **Primary Platforms**: Full episodes on main channels
- **Social Media**: Carefully selected authentic moments
- **Promotional Approach**: Focus on story themes, not guest promotion
- **Audience Preparation**: Set expectations for intimate format
