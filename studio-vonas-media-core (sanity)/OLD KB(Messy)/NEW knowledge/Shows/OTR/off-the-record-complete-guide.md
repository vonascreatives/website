# Off The Record (OTR) - Complete Production Guide

## Description
Comprehensive guide for Off The Record show production including show format, guest acquisition, OTR 2.0 documentary expansion, freelancer management, and complete operational workflows.

## Steps
1. Understand OTR's core mission and authentic storytelling approach
2. Learn guest acquisition and outreach strategies
3. Master OTR 2.0 documentary production workflow
4. Implement freelancer management for research and writing
5. Execute authentic interview and production protocols

## Content

### Show Mission and Core Identity

#### **Core Value Statement**
Off The Record is dedicated to featuring individuals often marginalized by society, including rebels, those involved in controversial activities, and individuals with extraordinary life stories. The core value is to look beyond surface judgments and explore the depth of the human experience, understanding that there is more to a person than meets the eye.

#### **Show Philosophy**
- Challenge societal norms and encourage empathy
- Reveal shared human experiences that connect us all
- Provide platform for voices typically unheard
- Focus on authentic storytelling over sensationalism
- Build understanding through genuine conversation

### OTR 2.0 Documentary Expansion

#### **New Format Overview**
OTR 2.0 represents evolution into documentary-style content featuring hidden or controversial topics within the Philippine context, produced with freelance researchers and writers for international audience appeal.

#### **Content Criteria**
- **Individual Interview Potential**: Each guest must provide compelling 30-minute interviews
- **Overall Topic Appeal**: Main topic interesting enough for multiple interviews  
- **Philippine-Centered Stories**: All content revolves around Philippine context
- **International Audience**: Content designed for global appeal
- **Secret/Hidden Angles**: Focus on underreported or controversial elements

#### **Reference Style**
- Format inspiration from Fern TV documentary approach
- Professional documentary production values
- Investigative journalism elements
- Multi-perspective storytelling structure

### Freelancer Management System

#### **Phase 1: Research & Guest Acquisition**
**Compensation Structure:**
- Payment per successfully secured guest who is approved for filming
- Example rates: 2,000 PHP per approved guest
- Potential for 10+ guests in 2 weeks = 20,000+ PHP
- Performance-based scaling opportunities

**Freelancer Responsibilities:**
1. **Topic Development**: Pitch compelling hidden/secret topics relevant to Philippines
2. **Deep Research**: Comprehensive analysis and fact verification
3. **Guest Outreach**: Pre-interview, confirm interest, ensure story value
4. **Quality Control**: Verify authenticity and interview potential
5. **Handoff Process**: Transfer approved guests to Producer

#### **Phase 2: Scriptwriting** 
**Project-Based Compensation:**
- Fees negotiated once documentary pitch approved
- 20-30 minute documentary format targeting
- Integration of interview transcripts and research
- Collaboration with AI-generated highlights and content suggestions

**Script Requirements:**
- **Introduction**: Hook with secret/controversial angle
- **Conflict/Climax**: Present tension or main challenge
- **Resolution/Insights**: Summarize findings and raise questions
- **Narrative Flow**: Compelling 20-30 minute documentary structure

### Guest Acquisition Strategy

#### **Target Guest Categories**
- **Social Issues**: Individuals affected by or involved in social problems
- **Survival Stories**: People who overcame significant challenges
- **Unique Conditions**: Those with rare experiences or perspectives
- **Inspiring Transformations**: Personal growth and change stories
- **Controversial Topics**: Subjects typically avoided by mainstream media

#### **Outreach Network Strategy**
**Personal Networks (Primary Source):**
- Friends, family, neighbors with interesting stories
- Community connections through barangay, church, school
- One connection leads to multiple connections (gang member knows other gang members)
- Local support groups, hobby clubs, workplaces

**Online Communities:**
- Facebook groups related to themes
- Reddit subreddits with relevant discussions
- AI tools (ChatGPT/Perplexity) for message crafting
- Social media networks and communities

#### **Location Priority**
- **Primary**: Metro Manila (San Juan studio access)
- **Secondary**: Cebu and Davao (possible remote production)
- **Approach**: Network-based thinking where one guest leads to multiple guests

### Production Workflow

#### **Guest Preparation Process**
1. **Initial Contact**: Personal, authentic outreach
2. **Pre-Interview**: 15-30 minute conversation to assess depth
3. **Story Verification**: Cross-check background and claims
4. **Comfort Assessment**: Ensure guest comfortable with format
5. **Consent Process**: Clear explanation and signed releases

#### **Recording Protocols**
- **Duration**: 45-90 minutes depending on story complexity
- **Environment**: Intimate, comfortable setting encouraging vulnerability  
- **Approach**: Conversational rather than interrogational
- **Technical**: Professional audio/video with multiple backups
- **Follow-up**: Post-recording comfort check and feedback

#### **Content Guidelines**
**Encouraged Elements:**
- Personal journey and authentic emotions
- Behind-the-scenes perspectives on social issues
- Lessons learned from difficult experiences
- Unique insights on common challenges

**Restrictions:**
- No unverified claims or allegations
- Respect privacy boundaries of third parties
- Avoid excessive trauma details without purpose
- Maintain dignity while exploring difficult topics

### OTR 2.0 Topic Development

#### **Successful Topic Examples**
- **"OnlyFans: The Hidden Secrets"** - Unseen economy in Philippines
- **"Missing Seamen"** - Underreported issue affecting Filipino families  
- **"Gambling Addiction in the Philippines"** - Cost and impact on society
- **Drug War Economics** - Financial and social impact analysis

#### **Topic Selection Criteria**
1. **Identify Strong "Secret" Angle**: Hidden, controversial, or underexplored elements
2. **Localize Context**: Philippine political, cultural, economic, or social nuances
3. **Feasibility Check**: Sufficient information and guest access available
4. **Revelation Potential**: Can uncover something new or shocking
5. **Expert Access**: Ability to secure credible interviewees

#### **Pitch Development**
- 1-2 paragraph clear, concise explanation
- Why topic is important and timely
- Potential guests identified with access strategy
- Unique angle or revelation potential highlighted

### Production Timeline

#### **Standard Timeline (9 weeks total)**
- **Weeks 1-4**: Pre-production (research, guest acquisition, planning)
- **Weeks 5-7**: Production (interviews, B-roll, supplementary content)
- **Weeks 8-9**: Post-production (editing, review, final delivery)

#### **Accelerated Timeline**
- **Full-time work**: Can reduce pre-production to 2 weeks
- **Guest availability**: Major variable affecting timeline
- **Contingency planning**: Backup guests and alternative angles ready

### Quality Standards

#### **Editorial Standards**
- Factual accuracy and verification required
- Ethical storytelling respecting participant dignity
- Legal review for sensitive or controversial content
- Guest approval process for personal story elements
- Professional journalism standards maintained

#### **Production Quality**
- Broadcast-quality audio and video
- Professional lighting and cinematography
- Clear, engaging narrative structure
- Appropriate pacing for documentary format
- High-quality post-production and finishing
