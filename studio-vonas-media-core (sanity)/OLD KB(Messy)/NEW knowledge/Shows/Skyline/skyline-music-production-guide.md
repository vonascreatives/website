# Skyline Music - Complete Production Guide

## Description
Comprehensive production guide for Skyline Music channel, the premiere destination for Filipino indie music featuring artist spotlights, live sessions, interviews, and artist database management.

## Steps
1. Understand Skyline Music's mission to spotlight Filipino indie talent
2. Learn artist discovery and database management processes
3. Master live session and interview production workflows
4. Implement artist outreach and collaboration strategies
5. Execute content creation and distribution protocols

## Content

### Channel Mission and Identity

#### **Channel Vision**
Welcome to Skyline, your premiere destination on YouTube for discovering the vibrant and diverse sounds of the Filipino indie music scene! Dive deep into the heart of the Philippines' rich musical landscape with exclusive artist spotlights, captivating live sessions, and in-depth interviews.

#### **Core Objectives**
- Spotlight Filipino indie artists and hidden gems
- Provide platform for emerging and established indie talent
- Connect artists with broader audiences locally and internationally
- Celebrate diversity and creativity of Filipino music scene
- Bridge gap between independent artists and mainstream recognition

#### **Target Audience**
- Filipino music enthusiasts seeking new discoveries
- Independent artists looking for exposure and collaboration
- International audiences interested in Philippine music culture
- Music industry professionals and talent scouts
- Creative community supporting independent artistry

### Artist Discovery and Database Management

#### **Artist Database System**
Skyline maintains comprehensive artist database for featured talent and potential collaborations through integrated Airtable system.

#### **Artist Registration Process**
**For Filipino indie artists seeking features:**
1. Visit Skyline website artist database signup
2. Complete detailed artist profile including:
   - Musical style and influences
   - Performance history and achievements
   - Sample tracks and portfolio materials
   - Contact information and availability
   - Social media presence and metrics

#### **Selection Criteria**
**Ideal Artist Profile:**
- Original Filipino indie music creation
- Professional or semi-professional performance quality  
- Compelling personal or artistic story
- Active engagement with music community
- Willing to participate in various content formats
- Available for live sessions and interviews

#### **Content Format Options**
- **Artist Spotlights**: 10-15 minute feature videos
- **Live Sessions**: Acoustic or full band performances
- **In-Depth Interviews**: 30-45 minute conversations
- **Collaborative Sessions**: Multi-artist projects
- **Behind-the-Scenes**: Studio visits and creative process

### Production Workflows

#### **Artist Spotlight Production**
**Pre-Production (1-2 weeks):**
- Artist research and background preparation
- Song selection and performance planning
- Location scouting (studio vs. unique venues)
- Technical requirements assessment
- Interview questions and narrative development

**Production Day:**
- Multi-camera setup for dynamic coverage
- Professional audio recording (live and backup)
- Performance capture with multiple angles
- Interview segments with authentic conversation
- B-roll footage of artist and environment

**Post-Production:**
- Multi-track audio mixing and mastering
- Video editing with engaging pacing
- Color grading for consistent visual style
- Graphics integration (artist info, song titles)
- Final review and artist approval process

#### **Live Session Format**
**Setup Requirements:**
- Intimate studio or acoustic setting
- Multiple camera angles focusing on performance
- High-quality audio capture (instruments and vocals)
- Lighting optimized for warm, authentic atmosphere
- Minimal production interference during performance

**Performance Guidelines:**
- 2-4 songs typically per session
- Mix of popular tracks and new material
- Acoustic or stripped-down arrangements preferred
- Natural interaction between songs encouraged
- Artist introduction and brief story sharing

#### **Interview Production**
**Interview Structure:**
- **Background**: Artist journey and musical development
- **Creative Process**: Songwriting and artistic inspiration
- **Filipino Music Scene**: Perspectives on indie landscape
- **Future Projects**: Upcoming releases and collaborations
- **Personal Story**: Authentic moments and experiences

**Technical Setup:**
- Comfortable, conversational environment
- Professional audio capture for both participants
- Multiple camera angles for dynamic editing
- Consistent lighting throughout interview
- Backup recording systems for security
