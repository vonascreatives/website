# Tools and Systems FAQs

## Description
Comprehensive frequently asked questions covering all tools and systems used at VONAS Media including software, automation, databases, and technical platforms.

## Content

### Software and Editing Tools

#### Q: What video editing software do we primarily use?
**A:** Adobe Premiere Pro is our primary editing platform, with DaVinci Resolve for advanced color grading and Final Cut Pro for specific workflow requirements. All editors are trained on multiple platforms to ensure flexibility and continuity.

#### Q: Do we have licenses for all the software we use?
**A:** Yes, we maintain current licenses for all professional software including Adobe Creative Cloud, Microsoft Office Suite, project management platforms, and specialized tools. License management is handled by IT with regular renewal tracking.

#### Q: Can I install additional software or plugins on work computers?
**A:** Software installation requires IT approval to ensure compatibility, licensing compliance, and security standards. Submit requests through the IT portal with business justification and technical requirements.

#### Q: What should I do if software crashes or becomes unresponsive?
**A:** First save any work if possible, then restart the application. If problems persist, contact IT support with specific error messages and steps to reproduce the issue. Critical software issues receive priority support.

#### Q: How do we handle software updates and new versions?
**A:** Updates are tested in controlled environments before company-wide deployment. Critical security updates are applied immediately, while major version updates follow a planned rollout schedule to minimize workflow disruption.

### Project Management and Collaboration

#### Q: Which project management system should I use for different types of projects?
**A:** Use Airtable for CRM and client relationship management, SmartSuite for complex project workflows, and ClickUp for task management and team coordination. Specific platform choice depends on project complexity and stakeholder requirements.

#### Q: How do I get access to project management platforms?
**A:** Access is granted through your supervisor or IT department based on role requirements. Complete platform-specific training before receiving full access to ensure proper usage and data security.

#### Q: What's the standard process for sharing files and collaborating on projects?
**A:** Use shared drives for active projects, version control naming conventions for file management, and project management platforms for task coordination. Large files should be shared through approved cloud storage platforms.

#### Q: How do I track time spent on different projects or tasks?
**A:** Time tracking is handled through integrated features in project management platforms or dedicated time tracking software as assigned. Regular time entry ensures accurate project cost tracking and resource allocation.

### Automation and Workflow Tools

#### Q: What automation tools are available to streamline repetitive tasks?
**A:** We use Zapier for cross-platform automation, n8n for complex workflow automation, Bardeen for browser-based automation, and built-in automation features in platforms like Airtable and SmartSuite.

#### Q: Can I create my own automation workflows?
**A:** Simple automations can be created with supervisor approval and training. Complex automations require IT review to ensure system stability and data security. Document all automations for team knowledge and maintenance.

#### Q: How do I troubleshoot automation workflows that aren't working?
**A:** Check automation logs for error messages, verify data formats and field mappings, ensure all connected platforms are functioning properly, and contact IT support for complex automation issues.

#### Q: Are there templates available for common automation scenarios?
**A:** Yes, we maintain a library of tested automation templates for common workflows like lead management, content scheduling, and file organization. Access templates through the automation portal with proper training.

### Database and CRM Systems

#### Q: How do I add new contacts to our CRM system?
**A:** Use standardized data entry procedures, check for existing contacts to avoid duplicates, ensure all required fields are completed, and follow up with appropriate automation triggers. Training materials cover detailed entry procedures.

#### Q: What's the policy for data quality and cleanliness in our databases?
**A:** Maintain accurate, up-to-date information, use consistent formatting and naming conventions, regularly review and clean your assigned data, and report data quality issues to the data management team.

#### Q: Can I export data from our systems for analysis or reporting?
**A:** Data exports require approval based on data sensitivity and intended use. Standard reports are available through system dashboards, while custom exports need justification and may require IT assistance.

#### Q: How do we ensure data security and privacy compliance?
**A:** Follow established access controls and user permissions, never share login credentials, report security incidents immediately, and complete required training on data privacy and security protocols.

### Communication and Scheduling Tools

#### Q: What's our primary communication platform for internal team communication?
**A:** Slack is our primary internal communication platform with organized channels for different teams, projects, and topics. Email is used for formal communication and external contacts.

#### Q: How should I organize my Slack channels and notifications?
**A:** Join relevant channels based on your role and projects, customize notification settings to balance awareness with productivity, use threads for detailed discussions, and follow channel-specific communication guidelines.

#### Q: What calendar system do we use for scheduling meetings and production?
**A:** Google Calendar is integrated across all systems with shared calendars for team coordination, production scheduling, and resource booking. Maintain up-to-date availability for efficient scheduling.

#### Q: How do I schedule meetings with external guests or clients?
**A:** Use approved scheduling tools like Calendly for guest coordination, follow protocol for guest communication and confirmation, coordinate with production team for technical requirements, and maintain professional scheduling etiquette.

### Content Creation and Design Tools

#### Q: What design software and resources are available for creating graphics and marketing materials?
**A:** Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, InDesign), Canva Pro for quick graphics, Figma for collaborative design, and access to stock photo and asset libraries through approved platforms.

#### Q: Are there brand guidelines and templates I should use?
**A:** Yes, comprehensive brand guidelines are available with approved templates, color palettes, fonts, and logo usage guidelines. All creative work must adhere to brand standards for consistency.

#### Q: How do I access stock photos, music, and other creative assets?
**A:** We have subscriptions to stock asset platforms with shared login credentials available through the creative team. Always verify licensing rights and usage permissions before using any assets.

#### Q: What's the approval process for creative work and marketing materials?
**A:** Creative work follows established approval workflows through project management systems, with different approval levels based on content type and distribution scope. Allow adequate time for review cycles.

### Technical Support and Troubleshooting

#### Q: Who do I contact for technical support with tools and software?
**A:** IT support is available through Slack (#tech-support), email (it@vonas-media.com), or phone during business hours. Include specific error messages and steps to reproduce issues for faster resolution.

#### Q: What information should I provide when reporting technical issues?
**A:** Include specific error messages or symptoms, steps taken before the issue occurred, software versions and operating system, screenshots if applicable, and urgency level of the issue.

#### Q: How quickly can I expect technical support response?
**A:** Critical issues affecting production receive immediate attention, standard support requests are addressed within 4 business hours, and non-urgent issues within 24 hours. Emergency support is available for production days.

#### Q: Are there self-help resources available for common technical issues?
**A:** Yes, we maintain a knowledge base with troubleshooting guides, video tutorials, and step-by-step solutions for common issues. Check self-help resources before submitting support tickets.

### Training and Learning Resources

#### Q: What training is available for new tools and software?
**A:** We provide comprehensive onboarding training, ongoing skill development workshops, access to online learning platforms, and mentorship for complex tools. Training plans are customized based on role requirements.

#### Q: How do I request additional training or access to learning resources?
**A:** Submit training requests through your supervisor with business justification and specific learning objectives. We support professional development that enhances job performance and career growth.

#### Q: Are there certifications or credentials I should pursue?
**A:** Industry-relevant certifications are encouraged and often supported financially. Discuss certification goals with your supervisor to align with career development and business needs.

### Integration and Compatibility

#### Q: How do our different tools and systems integrate with each other?
**A:** Most tools are integrated through APIs and automation platforms, enabling seamless data flow between systems. Integration maps are available showing how data flows between platforms.

#### Q: What should I do if I notice integration issues or data sync problems?
**A:** Report integration issues immediately as they can affect multiple workflows and team members. Provide specific examples of data discrepancies and affected systems for faster diagnosis.

#### Q: Can I suggest new tools or improvements to existing systems?
**A:** Yes, we welcome suggestions for workflow improvements and new tools. Submit suggestions through the innovation portal with business case, cost analysis, and implementation considerations.

### Security and Access Management

#### Q: How are user permissions and access levels determined?
**A:** Access is granted based on role requirements following principle of least privilege, with regular reviews and updates as roles change. All access is documented and audited for compliance.

#### Q: What's the policy for sharing login credentials or system access?
**A:** Never share login credentials under any circumstances. Individual accounts ensure accountability and security. Request additional user accounts through IT for team members needing access.

#### Q: How often should I change my passwords?
**A:** Follow company password policy requiring regular updates, use strong passwords with multi-factor authentication where available, and use approved password managers for secure password storage.
