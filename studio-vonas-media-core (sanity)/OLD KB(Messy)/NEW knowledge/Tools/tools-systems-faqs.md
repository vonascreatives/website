### Video Editing and Production Tools

#### **Q: What video editing software does VONAS Media use?**
**A:** Adobe Premiere Pro is our primary editing platform, with DaVinci Resolve for advanced color grading and Final Cut Pro for specific workflow requirements. All editors are trained on multiple platforms to ensure flexibility and continuity.

#### **Q: How do we use Klap for content creation?**
**A:** Klap is used for automated short-form content generation from long-form videos. Upload full episodes, configure AI settings for clip selection, apply brand-consistent styling, and generate platform-optimized clips for social media distribution. All generated clips go through quality review before publication.

#### **Q: What is Opus Clip used for?**
**A:** Opus Clip provides AI-powered video clipping with viral potential scoring. It analyzes long-form content, identifies engaging segments, and generates optimized clips with captions and branding. Use it for creating high-engagement social media content from podcast episodes and interviews.

#### **Q: How do we handle DaVinci Resolve projects?**
**A:** DaVinci Resolve is used primarily for color grading and music video editing. Create standardized project structures, use proxy workflows for efficiency, maintain consistent color management, and export using platform-specific presets. All projects include proper file organization and archive procedures.

#### **Q: What export settings should we use for different platforms?**
**A:** Platform-specific presets are configured in Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve. YouTube uses H.264 with high bitrate, TikTok requires 9:16 aspect ratio with specific compression, Instagram has multiple format requirements. All presets are documented in the video production workflow guide.

### Project Management and Database Systems

#### **Q: When should we use Airtable vs SmartSuite?**
**A:** Use Airtable for CRM and client relationship management, SmartSuite for complex project workflows, and ClickUp for task management and team coordination. Specific platform choice depends on project complexity and stakeholder requirements.

#### **Q: How do we organize data in SmartSuite?**
**A:** SmartSuite uses Solutions (high-level containers), Apps (specific databases), Tables (data storage), Views (filtered displays), and Workflows (automation). Organize by business function with Content Production, Business Operations, Technical Systems, and Marketing Solutions.

#### **Q: How does our guest database workflow work?**
**A:** Guest submissions trigger automated Airtable workflows: form submission → prefill link generation → email automation → additional info collection → release form distribution → database update → production ready status. Brandon Baquiran manages this system.

#### **Q: How do we integrate ClickUp with other systems?**
**A:** ClickUp integrates with SmartSuite for project synchronization, Make.com for workflow automation, and Slack for team notifications. Tasks auto-generate from topic development in Airtable, and project timelines sync across platforms for unified project management.

#### **Q: What's our backup plan for database systems?**
**A:** All databases have automated backup procedures with both cloud and local redundancy. Critical data is synchronized across multiple platforms (Airtable, SmartSuite, ClickUp) with export procedures and disaster recovery protocols documented in system management guides.

### Automation and Workflow Tools

#### **Q: What automation tools do we use?**
**A:** We use Zapier for cross-platform automation, n8n for complex workflow automation, Bardeen for browser-based automation, and built-in automation features in platforms like Airtable and SmartSuite.

#### **Q: How does Make.com to ActivePieces migration work?**
**A:** We're migrating simple workflows from Make.com (20k tokens) to ActivePieces (200k tokens) for better capacity. Complex multi-step processes remain on Make.com while basic automations move to ActivePieces for cost efficiency and increased processing power.

#### **Q: How does Bardeen help our workflows?**
**A:** Bardeen automates browser-based tasks like social media research, guest outreach, LinkedIn data extraction, and form filling. It connects with our CRM systems to automatically populate guest information and streamline manual research processes.

#### **Q: What triggers our automated workflows?**
**A:** Common triggers include: new Airtable form submissions, SmartSuite project updates, calendar events, email responses, social media mentions, and scheduled time-based triggers. Each trigger connects to specific response workflows documented in our automation guides.

#### **Q: How do we monitor automation performance?**
**A:** We track execution success rates, time savings, error frequencies, and ROI through dashboard analytics. Regular performance reviews identify optimization opportunities and system improvements for maximum efficiency.

### Creative and Design Tools

#### **Q: What design software licenses does the team have?**
**A:** Yes, we maintain current licenses for all professional software including Adobe Creative Cloud, Microsoft Office Suite, project management platforms, and specialized tools. License management is handled by IT with regular renewal tracking.

#### **Q: What design tools do we use for graphics?**
**A:** Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, InDesign), Canva Pro for quick graphics, Figma for collaborative design, and access to stock photo and asset libraries through approved platforms.

#### **Q: How do we maintain brand consistency across tools?**
**A:** Creative Cloud Libraries contain all brand assets, color palettes, fonts, and templates. Standardized templates are available for each show, and brand guidelines are enforced through automated checking and team training.

#### **Q: Where are our design templates stored?**
**A:** All templates are centralized in Adobe Creative Cloud Libraries with show-specific collections. Canva templates are organized by show and content type. Access permissions are role-based with shared team libraries for collaboration.

#### **Q: How do we handle client design revisions?**
**A:** Version control is maintained through Creative Cloud with clear naming conventions. All revisions are tracked with timestamps and comments. Client feedback is incorporated systematically with approval workflows documented in project management systems.

### Communication and Collaboration Tools

#### **Q: How is our Slack workspace organized?**
**A:** Channels are organized by function: General (company-wide), Show Production (per show), Department (by team), Project (temporary), and Integration (system notifications). Each channel has specific purposes and communication protocols.

#### **Q: What are our team communication standards?**
**A:** Professional tone, 2-hour response time for urgent messages, 24-hour for general messages, strategic use of @mentions, organized thread discussions, and respect for time zones and after-hours boundaries.

#### **Q: How do we use Slack for project coordination?**
**A:** Project-specific channels coordinate cross-team collaboration, automated notifications from project management systems provide real-time updates, and integration with calendars and task management ensures comprehensive coordination.

#### **Q: What video conferencing tools do we use?**
**A:** Primary platforms include Zoom for client meetings, Slack Huddles for quick team coordination, Google Meet for casual discussions, and professional video conferencing setup for podcast recordings and interviews.

#### **Q: How do we share files and collaborate on documents?**
**A:** Google Workspace for document collaboration, Adobe Creative Cloud for design assets, Dropbox for large file sharing, and Slack for quick file sharing with version control and organized project folders.

### Troubleshooting and Support

#### **Q: What do we do when automation fails?**
**A:** Check system status and connectivity, review error logs and trigger conditions, verify authentication and permissions, test with manual processes, contact vendor support if needed, and implement backup procedures while resolving issues.

#### **Q: How do we handle software conflicts and compatibility issues?**
**A:** Document all system configurations, test updates in sandbox environments, maintain compatibility matrices, have rollback procedures ready, coordinate with IT support, and communicate changes to affected team members.

#### **Q: What's our disaster recovery plan for tools and systems?**
**A:** Complete data backup and recovery procedures, alternative tool access and workflows, emergency communication protocols, vendor support contact information, and business continuity plans with regular testing.

#### **Q: How do we report and resolve technical issues?**
**A:** Structured issue reporting through designated channels, priority classification and assignment, documented troubleshooting procedures, escalation protocols, and resolution tracking with post-incident analysis.

#### **Q: Where can we find help and documentation?**
**A:** Internal knowledge base with comprehensive guides, vendor documentation and support resources, team expertise directory, training materials and video tutorials, and external support contacts with clear escalation procedures.
