# VONAS Media Internal Wiki - Raw Content

## Source
URL: https://vonass-organization.gitbook.io/vonas-media-internal-wiki/

## Structure Overview

### 1. About Us
- **About V O N A S**: Company overview and introduction
- **Vision, Mission & Purpose**: 
  - Vision: Company's future aspirations
  - Mission: Core purpose and goals
  - Purpose: Fundamental reason for existence
- **Values**: Core organizational values

### 2. Team
- **Meet the Team**: Team member profiles and roles
- **The V O N A S Slack**: Internal communication guidelines and setup

### 3. Interns (Most Extensive Section)
- **Intern Essentials**: Core information for interns
- **General Onboarding**: Initial orientation process
- **Intern Handbook**: Comprehensive guide for interns
- **Internship Program**: Program structure and expectations
- **Intern's Bible** (Detailed Guides):
  - Marketing Processes: Step-by-step marketing workflows
  - Social Media Management: Content creation and posting guidelines
  - Guest Selection: Process for selecting podcast/video guests
  - Video Distribution: How to distribute video content across platforms
  - Production Workflows: Video and content production processes

### 4. Freelancers
- **Work Collaboration Guidelines**: How to work with external freelancers
- **Rates**: Payment structures and rate information
- **Specific Guides for**:
  - Videographers: Guidelines and expectations for video professionals
  - Graphic Designers: Design standards and workflow processes
  - Editors: Video and content editing guidelines

### 5. Policies
- **Time Off Requesting**: Process for requesting vacation and sick time

## Key Characteristics
- Comprehensive internal documentation
- Structured for onboarding and ongoing team reference
- Emoji-based navigation system
- Covers operational details across all departments
- Focus on practical, actionable information
