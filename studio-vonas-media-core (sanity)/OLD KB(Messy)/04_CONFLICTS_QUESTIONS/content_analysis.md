#### 3. Tools and Systems
**Internal Wiki**: 
- Limited tool documentation (mainly in freelancer guides)

**Core Team Wiki**: 
- Extensive tool documentation (ClickConnector, Klap, Opus Clip, etc.)

**Potential Overlap**: Minimal, Core Team wiki appears more comprehensive for tools

### Content Gaps Identified

#### 1. Audience Targeting
- **Internal Wiki**: Focuses heavily on interns and freelancers
- **Core Team Wiki**: Focuses on core team operations
- **Gap**: No clear bridge between intern processes and core team workflows

#### 2. Permission and Access Levels
- Different content depth suggests different access needs
- **Question**: Should interns have access to core team operational details?

#### 3. Show-Specific Information
- **Core Team Wiki**: Has detailed show documentation (Passions, Off the Record, etc.)
- **Internal Wiki**: Limited show-specific information
- **Potential Issue**: Interns may need show-specific guidelines

### Categorization Strategy

#### Business Operations
- Company vision, mission, values
- Team structure and roles
- Organizational policies

#### Content Production
- Video production workflows
- Social media management
- Content distribution processes
- Show-specific procedures

#### Team Management
- Onboarding processes (intern vs core team)
- Collaboration guidelines
- Communication protocols
- Performance management

#### Tools and Systems
- Software usage guidelines
- Database management (SmartSuite, Airtable)
- Automation tools (Bardeen, etc.)
- Production tools (Klap, Opus Clip, etc.)

#### External Relations
- Freelancer management
- Guest selection and management
- Brand outreach and CRM

### Recommended Consolidation Approach

1. **Create Master Categories** with sub-levels for different audiences
2. **Maintain Separate Access Levels** but unified content structure
3. **Cross-Reference Related Content** to avoid true duplication
4. **Standardize Process Documentation** across both wikis

### Questions for Clarification

2. Which version of overlapping processes is the most current/accurate?
3. Are there processes that need different detail levels for different audiences?
4. Should show-specific information be accessible to interns?
